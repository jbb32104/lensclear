How does the self-cleaning system work?+

The concept uses an ultrasonic actuator to create high-frequency vibrations at the optical surface. This motion is designed to help dislodge loose dust without wiping the glass. The animation above illustrates how the components fit together.

Does it replace all manual cleaning?+

No. Self-cleaning is intended to help with loose dust. Fingerprints, oils, and stubborn debris may still need appropriate manual care. Final cleaning guidance will accompany the product.

Will LensClear One fit my camera?+

Camera mounts and compatibility are still being developed. We’ll share the supported systems and final specifications before the product becomes available.

When can I get one?+

LensClear One is in development. Pricing and launch timing have not yet been announced. You can download the product overview below for a closer look at the concept.
