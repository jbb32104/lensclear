LENSCLEAR ONE
Clarity, uninterrupted.

PRODUCT CONCEPT
A self-cleaning camera lens designed to help keep loose dust from interrupting the shot.

HOW IT WORKS
The proposed system combines a front optical element, an ultrasonic actuator, a precision lens housing, and a camera mount. The actuator creates high-frequency vibrations at the optical surface to help dislodge loose dust.

DESIGN INTENT
Integrate optical performance and a cleaning mechanism in a single assembly, reducing interruptions to photography.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS
LensClear One is a concept in development. Renderings and the exploded view are illustrative. Final performance, compatibility, power requirements, dimensions, pricing, and launch timing have not been established. Self-cleaning does not remove the need for all manual cleaning, particularly fingerprints, oils, and stubborn debris.
